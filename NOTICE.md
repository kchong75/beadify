# Third-party material

## MARD bead palette (`src/beadify/palettes/mard.csv`)

Color codes and RGB values come from the `raw/mard.csv` data of
[maxcleme/beadcolors](https://github.com/maxcleme/beadcolors), which is distributed under the MIT License
(https://beadcolors.eremes.xyz/raw/mard.csv). Only the `code,r,g,b` columns are kept.
The RGB values are screen approximations and do not exactly match physical beads.

## DejaVu fonts (`src/beadify/fonts/`)

`DejaVuSans.ttf` and `DejaVuSans-Bold.ttf` are bundled unmodified so charts render identically on every
machine. They are distributed under the DejaVu / Bitstream Vera license; see `src/beadify/fonts/LICENSE_DEJAVU`.
